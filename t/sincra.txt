  [1m"Doctors shouldn't pay interest on money that's already theirs."[0m

  In Brazil a surgeon can wait months to be paid for an operation, and
  the hospital often can't tell where that money is stuck: internal
  audit, the insurer, a missing signature. Sincra rebuilds every
  procedure's path from surgery to payout and predicts how much each
  doctor will receive, when, and with what confidence.
